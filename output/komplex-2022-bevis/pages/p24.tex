\[ f(z) = \prod_{i = 1}^N (z - a_i)^{n_i} \prod_{j = 1}^M (z - b_j)^{-m_j} g(z), \quad g \text{ holo och } \neq 0 \]

\[ \frac{f'(z)}{f(z)} = \sum_{i = 1}^N \frac{n_i}{z - a_i} - \sum_{j = 1}^M \frac{m_j}{z - b_j} + \frac{g'(z)}{g(z)}, \quad \frac{g'}{g} \text{ holo i } H \]
$\Rightarrow \dfrac{f'}{f}$ holo i $H$ förutom isol.\ sing.\ i $a_1, \dots, a_N, b_1, \dots, b_M$.
\begin{align*}

\end{align*}
