\textbf{Lösning:} $\arg z = \varphi + 2\pi k$ \ $k \in \mathbb{Z}$, \ $\operatorname{Arg} z = \varphi + 2\pi k$ s.a.\ $\operatorname{Arg} z \in (-\pi, \pi]$

\begin{definition}
$\operatorname{Log} z = \ln|z| + i\operatorname{Arg} z$, \ $\log z$
\end{definition}
\begin{definition}
$a^b = e^{b \cdot \operatorname{Log} a}$ \qquad $i^i = (e^{\pi i/2})^i = \dots = e^{-\pi/2}$

''$(e^{\pi i/2 + 2\pi i})^i$'' $= e^{-\pi/2 - 2\pi}$ \ \textcolor{magenta!80!black}{$\leftarrow$ fel pga ej hänsyn för komplexa tal}
\end{definition}

a) $\ln|z| + i\operatorname{Arg} z = \pi i/2$
\[ \Rightarrow \begin{cases} \ln|z| = 0 \\ \operatorname{Arg} z = \pi/2 \end{cases} \Rightarrow \begin{cases} |z| = 1 \\ \operatorname{Arg} z = \pi/2 \end{cases} \Rightarrow z = i \]
b) $\ln|z| + i\operatorname{Arg} z = \dfrac{3\pi i}{2} \Rightarrow \begin{cases} \ln|z| = 0 \\ \operatorname{Arg} z = \frac{3\pi}{2} \end{cases}$
