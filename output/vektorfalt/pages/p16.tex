Gå i gräns på integralen:
\[ \lim_{\delta V \to 0} \frac{1}{\delta V} \frac{\partial u_1}{\partial x}\,\delta V = \frac{\partial u_1}{\partial x} \]

På samma sätt för resterande 4 ytor:\\
$\Rightarrow \dfrac{\partial u_2}{\partial y}$ och $\dfrac{\partial u_3}{\partial z}$. Lägg ihop alla termer
\[ \Rightarrow \operatorname{div}(\vb{u}) = \frac{\partial u_1}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial u_2}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_3}{\partial z} \]
Notation: $\operatorname{div}(\vb{u}) = \nabla \cdot \vb{u}$
\[ \nabla = \left( \frac{\partial}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right) \]
\begin{align*}
\Rightarrow \nabla \cdot \vb{u} &= \left( \frac{\partial}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \right) \cdot (u_1, u_2, u_3) \\
&= \frac{\partial u_1}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial u_2}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial u_3}{\partial z}
\end{align*}
